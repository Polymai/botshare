BotShare

Rent robots from people near you

BotShare is a peer-to-peer robot rental marketplace. Browse household helpers, garden robots, and work robots on the map, request the dates you need, and message the owner. Robot owners publish listings with photos, skills, and daily prices, and unlock renter conversations with a one-time fee per listing.

How BotShare works

  • Renters: browse published robots by task, price per day, and location, save favorites, and send a rental request with your dates.
  • Owners: create a listing with photos, skills, availability, and a map pin, then publish it to the marketplace.
  • Conversations: when requests arrive, owners unlock messaging for a listing with a one-time fee and agree on handover directly with the renter.

Frequently asked questions

What robots can I rent? Household assistants, lawn and garden robots, snow clearers, window cleaners, lifting helpers, and cooking robots listed by private owners.

How is price set? Every listing shows the owner's price per day. You agree on handover and duration directly with the owner.

How do I list my robot? Create a free account, add photos, skills, availability, and a pickup location, then publish. You pay a small one-time fee per listing to unlock renter conversations.
